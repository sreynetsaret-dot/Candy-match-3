Multi Tab Browser

View one website in multiple panels. Two columns with lazy loading.

Enter a website address and choose how many panels to display.
Important: Some websites block being displayed inside another website (iframe). This restriction is controlled by the website owner and cannot be bypassed here. If a panel is blank, tap “Open ↗” to open the site directly. Panels are loaded only as you scroll, with a limit of three simultaneous loads to reduce memory and connection pressure on phones. This tool does not auto-refresh pages or generate ad views or clicks.
